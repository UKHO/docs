# Outsourced Development Policy

## Objective

This policy defines minimum controls for engaging third parties to design, build, test, or maintain engineering assets.

## Policy

- Outsourced development must meet the same engineering security, quality, and evidence expectations as internal delivery.
- Contracts or work orders must define security, testing, review, and artefact expectations.
- Third-party access must be approved, least privilege, time-bound, and reviewable.
- Deliverables must remain subject to UKHO review, acceptance, and release controls.
- Source code, dependencies, secrets handling, and pipeline usage must comply with UKHO engineering policies.
- Evidence required for acceptance must be defined before delivery begins.
- Ownership of residual risk remains with the named UKHO risk owner and cannot be delegated to the supplier.

## Minimum evidence

- supplier access approvals
- acceptance criteria
- review outputs
- retained engineering artefacts and assurance evidence
