# Engineering Assurance Evidence Checklist

This checklist defines the minimum evidence engineering teams should be able to provide for audit, assurance, or internal review.

## Core evidence areas

- service owner and risk owner identified
- architecture or system context documentation
- security requirements or NFR records
- threat model or equivalent risk assessment
- source control and review evidence
- build, test, and security scan evidence
- deployment and approval records
- operational runbooks and support contacts
- logging, monitoring, and dashboard references
- backup, restore, and recovery evidence where relevant
- access review outputs
- risk acceptance and exception records
- incident review and lessons learned records where relevant

## Notes

- Evidence should be proportionate to service criticality and risk.
- Links to live systems or dashboards are acceptable where durable and access-controlled.
- Missing evidence should be treated as an assurance gap and tracked to closure.
