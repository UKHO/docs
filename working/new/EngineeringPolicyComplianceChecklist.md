# Engineering Policy Compliance Checklist

This checklist provides a lightweight way for teams to assess adoption of the engineering policy suite.

## Checklist

- Secure development roles and training are in place.
- Security requirements are documented for the service.
- Threat modelling or equivalent security design review exists.
- Source control, branch protection, and code review controls are active.
- Build, test, and security scanning controls are active.
- Logging and monitoring controls are implemented.
- Access reviews are being completed.
- Test data handling follows approved rules.
- Backup and restore responsibilities are defined.
- Runbooks and operational contacts exist.
- Production changes use controlled deployment methods.
- Exceptions and accepted risks are recorded.

## Outcome guidance

- **Compliant**: control implemented and evidenced
- **Partially compliant**: control present but incomplete or weak
- **Non-compliant**: control absent or not evidenced

Teams should review this checklist periodically and after material service changes.
