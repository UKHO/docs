# Non-Functional Requirements Policy

Non-functional requirements (NFRs) are everyone's responsibility. This policy applies to work undertaken by delivery teams and seeks to ensure that NFRs are present and delivered as part of that work.

## Requirements

- Delivery teams should ensure NFRs are present when starting new work. If they are not, the solution architect aligned with the team should be tasked with discovering and providing the relevant NFRs. If the team does not know who their solution architect is, they should consult the architecture practice head.
- The intended support team or Support Principal may have their own NFRs for the impacted services and should be consulted.
- NFRs may change as the solution progresses, but fulfilling them must not be de-prioritised or disregarded.
- Demonstrating that NFRs have been satisfied must be included as part of signing off a piece of work.
- When work is handed from a delivery team to a support team, the delivery team must respect the NFRs. The support team has authority to reject the delivery until its NFRs are satisfied.

For the [UKHO NFR definition](https://ukho.sharepoint.com/sites/eagwiki/Pages/NFR%27s.aspx), see the linked definition.
