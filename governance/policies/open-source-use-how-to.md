# Checking a Licence and Proposing an Addition

This guide explains how to check a third-party component's licence against the [Use of Free Open Source Software Policy](open-source-use.md) and what to do when the licence is not on the allowlist.

## Check the licence

1. Identify the licence under which the component was released.
2. Compare it with the [UKHO allowlist of approved licences](open-source-use-reference.md#permitted-licences).
3. If the licence is on the list, use the component subject to the policy requirements. Changes to third-party components must be reviewed by the team's Lead Engineer.

## Propose an unlisted licence

If the licence is not on the allowlist but appears reasonable, the team's Lead Engineer should propose adding it at the Lead Engineer's meeting. If further clarity is needed, consult TPE management and the Legal department.

A proposal does not itself change the allowlist. Changes to the allowlist must be agreed by the Software Engineering Team Manager and the UKHO Legal Advisor, as set out in the policy.

Verification is automated. Contact the DDC team to find out how this works.
