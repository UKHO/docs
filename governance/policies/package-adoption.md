# Package Adoption Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to packages that are orphaned, or at risk of
becoming orphaned, because of business change to teams, and to
packages that require adoption.

## Requirements

- Where possible, UKHO should reuse assets, including packages,
  rather than creating new ones.
- Packages that require adoption should be analysed to quantify the
  work involved in bringing them to an acceptable state for initial
  adoption and in maintaining them over time.
- Analysis outcomes should be recorded in an [adoption
  checklist](../../software-engineering-policies/PackageAdoption/
  PackageAdoptionChecklist.md).
- The analysis outcomes should be presented to the Support
  Committee and the package proposed for adoption.
- A team should accept the package for adoption and create stories
  based on the analysis outcomes.
- This applies to both currently orphaned packages and packages that
  may become orphaned in the future because of business change to
  teams.
