# Defect Management Policy

An effective Defect Management workflow must be in place for all teams.

Each team must create and maintain its Defect Management workflow, working with the Test Leads to ensure it meets the requirements below.

The workflow should include:

- A definition of a defect
- The minimum detail required when logging a defect
- Where, when and how defects should be logged
- A defect triage process, including whether the defect contains enough detail, whether it is a defect, its severity and when it should be fixed
- A defect closure process

Each team is responsible for ensuring that its Defect Management workflow is followed.
