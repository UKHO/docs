# Test Policy

This policy defines the testing requirements for new and updated software at UKHO. Our testing covers functional and non-functional areas as described in the [test strategy](../../quality-assurance/test-strategy.md). The policy is the authoritative statement for testing obligations and governance.

## Scope

This policy applies to testing of new and updated software.

## Requirements

- Testing must ensure that safety-related systems do not cause inaccuracy or delay to our products.
- Testing must support the good reputation of the organisation.
- Testing must support agile delivery by allowing frequent releases containing valuable changes for users.
- Testing activities must focus on the most valuable testing activities.
- The Test Practice consists of a group of test engineers who provide a testing capability across the UKHO software delivery teams. It also provides oversight of the testing activities of delivery partners.
- Where other members of the business carry out software testing, the Test Practice is available to offer help and advice.
- Software Engineering Team Managers line manage all test engineers. However, test engineers are task-managed by the delivery programs in conjunction with the Head of Software Engineering.
- A standard Test Approach document must be written at the start of developing the Beta product.
- A Test Summary Report must be created and signed off by stakeholders before the Beta is released.
- Test processes may differ by team or project, according to individual circumstances, but all test approaches must conform to the UKHO's [test strategy](../../quality-assurance/test-strategy.md).
- Live data must not be used for testing.
- Where a copy of live data is used, its use must be risk assessed and the data sanitised as appropriate, removing references to customers, employees and UKHO corporate proprietary information.
