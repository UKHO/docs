# Why We Test

This document explains the rationale behind the [Test Policy](test.md). The policy remains the authoritative source for requirements.

## Purpose of testing

The source sets out the goals for testing at UKHO. Testing helps ensure safety-related systems do not cause inaccuracy or delay to products. It also supports the organisation's reputation and helps support frequent releases containing valuable changes for users.

The policy also makes clear that testing effort should be focused on the most valuable activities. This helps avoid low-value work while still supporting delivery and risk reduction.

## Governance and responsibilities

The Test Practice provides a specialist testing capability across software delivery teams and oversight of delivery partners. Where other parts of the business carry out testing, the Practice remains available to offer help and advice.

Software Engineering Team Managers line manage test engineers, while delivery programs manage their day-to-day work with the Head of Software Engineering. This arrangement balances local delivery needs with specialist governance.

## Lifecycle and review

The source describes a standard testing lifecycle in which a Test Approach document is written at the start of developing a Beta product and a Test Summary Report is signed off by stakeholders before release. This supports a structured assessment of whether the product is ready and whether the testing evidence is adequate.

## Data handling

The policy does not allow live data to be used for testing. When a copy of live data is used, it must be risk assessed and sanitised so that references to customers, employees and UKHO corporate proprietary information are removed. This reduces the risk of exposing sensitive information while enabling realistic testing.
