# Discover and Deliver Non-Functional Requirements

Use this procedure when starting or delivering a piece of work. It applies the [Non-Functional Requirements Policy](nfr.md).

1. At the start of the work, check that the NFRs are present.
2. If they are not present, ask the solution architect aligned with the team to discover and provide the relevant NFRs. If you do not know who that architect is, consult the architecture practice head.
3. Consult the intended support team or Support Principal about their NFRs for the impacted services.
4. As the solution progresses, account for changes to the NFRs. Do not de-prioritise or disregard fulfilling them.
5. Before signing off the work, include a demonstration that the NFRs have been satisfied.
6. If the work is to be handed to a support team, respect its NFRs. The support team may reject the delivery until its NFRs are satisfied.
