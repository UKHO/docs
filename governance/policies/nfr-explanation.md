# Why Non-Functional Requirements Matter

This document explains the context of the [Non-Functional Requirements Policy](nfr.md).

## Purpose and scope

The policy seeks to ensure that NFRs are present and delivered as part of work undertaken by a delivery team. It treats NFRs as everyone's responsibility.

For the organisation's definition of NFRs, see the [UKHO NFR definition](https://ukho.sharepoint.com/sites/eagwiki/Pages/NFR%27s.aspx).

## Changing requirements and completion

NFRs may change as a solution progresses. The policy makes clear that this does not remove the requirement to fulfil them: they must not be de-prioritised or disregarded. Demonstrating that they have been satisfied is part of signing off the work.

## Support handover

The intended support team or Support Principal may have NFRs for the impacted services. When work is handed over, the delivery team must respect the NFRs, and the support team has authority to reject the delivery until its NFRs are satisfied.
